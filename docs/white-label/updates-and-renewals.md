# Updates and renewals

Every licence includes a period of releases from the purchase date. The [updates page](https://stratastorage.aoneahsan.com/white-label/updates) shows its length and the renewal prices.

## What an update is

- Bug fixes, adapter changes and new storage options in `strata-storage`, rebuilt into your package.
- Capacitor and framework support as the package adopts it, with the native plugin rebuilt.
- Your docs updated for each published release, migration notes included.

A change only your team needs isn't an update; it's custom work.

## When the period ends

Your package stays published at its last version and keeps working in your developers' apps. Renew for 3, 6 or 12 months with your licence key and licence email on the updates page to keep releases coming.
